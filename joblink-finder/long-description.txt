JobLink Finder is a privacy-first Chrome extension that helps job seekers discover hidden job postings across major applicant tracking systems and company career pages.

Instead of manually writing advanced Google search operators, JobLink Finder creates optimized ATS job searches in the background, scans Google result pages, extracts matching job links, removes duplicates, and saves results locally in your browser.

Built for job seekers who search across Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, iCIMS, SuccessFactors, Oracle Cloud, BambooHR, LinkedIn Jobs, and direct company career pages.

Key features:
• Search across major ATS platforms from one simple form
• Discover Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, and company career jobs
• Automatically extract job links from Google search results
• Save and track jobs locally in Chrome
• Choose how many job leads to save or open
• Update job status: Saved, Applied, Interview, Rejected
• Optional open-results-in-tabs mode
• Duplicate removal for cleaner job tracking
• No login, no backend, no analytics, no tracking

Privacy-first design:
All job data stays in chrome.storage.local on your device. JobLink Finder does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your job search data.

Ideal for:
• Job seekers
• Students and new graduates
• Workday/HRIS professionals
• Data engineers and software engineers
• Anyone searching company ATS job boards more efficiently

JobLink Finder helps you search smarter, save better job leads, and manage your application pipeline privately.
